# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release, for Minecraft 26.2 with Fabric. Scenes are recorded with Flashback or Replay Mod.

- **Editor:** Right Ctrl opens an editor over the world with an outliner, an inspector and a timeline with a curve
  editor. Place any vanilla mob, Mannequins as players, TNT and props; move and turn them with handles; key any
  channel; undo and redo everything. F1 lists every shortcut.
- **Actors** are vanilla entities with no mind of their own. With the editor closed they stay in view but nothing
  outside the scene can push, hit, shoot or ride them, and they are never saved with the world.
- **Motion paths** drawn or sketched on the ground snap to real blocks, with vanilla stepping, jumping and falling,
  speed keys, gaits and markers. They follow craters, even ones that open mid-walk.
- **Fights:** attacks are hit-tested like vanilla (reach, facing, sight, cooldown) and the hurt flashes, health,
  knockback and deaths are written onto the timeline. Rules can be set for the scene, each object and each event.
- **Explosions** break blocks with the vanilla ray algorithm, set off chain reactions, hurt and throw objects,
  and rewind completely. Shots are aimed and fly with vanilla drag and gravity.
- **Mob looks and animations:** charged creepers, angry wolves, rearing horses with saddles and armor, villager
  professions, screaming endermen, warden poses, a golem offering a flower and more, from data files.
- **Performance capture:** act a part out and keep it as keyframes or a motion path; loop capture builds crowds.
- **Crowds, groups, variables and conditional events, the scene track** (time, weather, sounds, commands), and
  **Record** for Flashback or Replay Mod.
